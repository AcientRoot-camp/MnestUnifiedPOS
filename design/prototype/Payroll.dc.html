UNDERSTANDING YOUR PAYSLIP
Earnings is everything you were paid before anything was taken off: your basic salary plus overtime, commission and any allowances. Added together these give your Gross Salary. Net Pay is your gross salary minus total deductions — the amount sent to the account shown on this payslip. Take Home Pay is the same figure.
Deductions are the amounts taken off your gross pay. PAYE / Income Tax is charged on your gross pay after SSNIT. Your SSNIT Pension Contribution is charged on basic salary only, not on allowances. A Salary advance or loan is a fixed monthly amount. Social Security Contribution shows both sides of your SSNIT pension. The employer's share is paid in by the company on top of your salary — it is not deducted from your pay and does not reduce your net pay. Your own share is the SSNIT deduction listed on the left.
This payslip is issued electronically and is valid without a signature. Please keep it for your records. If any figure looks wrong, contact {{ps.coName}} payroll quoting the reference shown at the top of this payslip.
